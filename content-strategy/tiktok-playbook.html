Your glossary earns respect from peers. But the people with budget — founders, heads of product and marketing at AI companies — don't buy theory. They buy relief from a pain they can name. This playbook keeps both: authority and demand, on the same feed.

The system · 4 pillars

Four pillars, two audiences

Each pillar has a job and an audience. Two speak to peers and build authority; two speak to buyers and create inbound. Balance the feed — don't let it drift all-theory (no leads) or all-pitch (no trust).

The path · hook to call

From a two-second hook to a discovery call

1

Hook — first 2 seconds

A pattern-interrupt in their language, straight from the Translation Engine. No intro, no logo, no “hey guys.”

2

Value — 15–40 seconds

One concrete insight or a live fix. Teach, don't pitch. The proof is the pitch.

3

Soft CTA

Give the algorithm a comment and give yourself a signal.“Comment AUDIT and I'll show you how to check yours.” / “Follow for the whole system.”

4

Profile — the one-liner that converts

Not “content strategist.” A promise.“I help AI & fintech teams make their AI sound human — and get found by AI search.”

5

Lead magnet

A free “Is your brand visible to AI?” self-check. Low-friction, high-signal — everyone who runs it is a warm lead. (Worth building next — it's a natural companion to your prospecting tool.)

6

DM → discovery → engagement

The DM is already warm because they came to you. Discovery call, scoped proposal, and it flows straight into the Prospecting Command Center you already have.
